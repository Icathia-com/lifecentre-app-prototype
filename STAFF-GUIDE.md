# Life Centre app: staff guide

This guide is for church staff, leaders and admins. You do not need any technical skills.

## Who can do what

| | Admin | Staff | Leader |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Post notices | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add verses | Yes | Yes | No |
| Schedule written devotions | Yes | Yes | No |
| Turn on "Full chapter for Biblica versions" | Yes | No | No |
| Create and edit events | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add a video to Watch | Yes | No | Yes |
| Remove a video from Watch | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Add or remove people on the staff list | Yes | No | No |
| Request a launch pop-up for an event | Yes | Yes | No |
| Approve pop-up requests | Yes | With "Can approve pop-ups" | With "Can approve pop-ups" |
| Make a pop-up live directly, and the pop-up master switch | Yes | No | No |
| Post stories | Yes | With "Can post stories" | With "Can post stories" |

## Sign in

1. Open the app and tap the round avatar at the top right of Home. This opens your **profile**. Tap the gear at its top right to
   open **Account**.
2. Tap **Staff sign-in with Google** and choose your Google account.
3. Account now shows your email and your role, with your tools underneath (**Admin tools** or **Staff tools**).

If it says **"Not on the staff list"**, ask an admin to add the email address you signed in with (see "Manage the staff list" below).
To leave, or to switch to another account, tap **Sign out** on Account.

To book a room you also sign in as a member (an emailed 6-digit code). Do that from **Sign in for bookings** on Account, or from Facilities booking.
The staff page is still at **https://icathia.com/lifecentre-app-prototype/app/#/staff**.

## Post a notice

Notices show in the app under the bell on the Home screen.

1. Sign in, then tap **Notices**.
2. Fill in the **Title** and the **Message**.
3. Check the **Date shown**. It is today by default.
4. If you want a button under the notice, add a **Link** (it must start with `https://`) and a **Link label** (for example "Register").
5. Tick **Published (visible to everyone)** to show it now. Leave it unticked to keep it as a draft that only staff can see.
6. Tap **Save**. You will see "Saved."

To change a notice, tap it in the list, edit it, and tap **Save**. To remove it, tap **Delete** next to it.

## Add a verse

You choose the passage. The app shows its text on the Home screen on the day you pick, in the Bible version each person has chosen (NIV to begin with, or the version chosen on that phone). If no passage is set for a day, the app uses the day's regular verse.

1. Sign in, then tap **Verses**.
2. Pick the **Date**, the **Book**, the **Chapter** and **Verse from**. For more than one verse, also fill in **Verse to**.
3. Tap **Save**.

A passage can be up to 25 verses. The list shows each verse by its date and reference only; the text is filled in for you, usually within the hour, and only for today and tomorrow.

To change a verse, tap it in the list, edit it, and tap **Save**. You cannot change its date; delete it and add a new one instead.

**The daily verse flow.** Tapping the verse on Home opens it full screen in the member's version. From there they swipe up (or tap
the arrow) to today's stories, or to today's written devotion when no story is up (see below), and then to their streak: how many
days in a row they reached the end. Without a story or a devotion the verse leads straight to the streak. Counting happens on each
phone, with no sign-in, and is never sent anywhere.

**Read the full chapter.** For most versions the verse screen offers **Read the full chapter**, with the day's verses highlighted.
The chapter is fetched by the hourly verse job, for today only, and kept for 7 days. For versions published by Biblica (NIV, the
Tamil, Malayalam, Thai and Chinese Contemporary versions) it is offered only once an admin ticks **Full chapter for Biblica
versions**, at the top of **Verses** on the staff page. Leave it off until the church has Biblica's permission.

## Schedule a devotion

A written devotion shows in the daily verse flow on any day when nobody has a story up.

1. Sign in, then tap **Devotions**.
2. Pick the **Date** (tomorrow by default) and fill in the **Title** and the **Devotion** (up to 3000 characters; a blank line
   starts a new paragraph).
3. Pick the **Book**, the **Chapter**, **Verse from** and, for more than one verse, **Verse to** (up to 25 verses).
4. Write the closing **Prayer**, then tap **Save**.

One devotion per date. To change one, tap **Edit** next to it in the list; to remove it, tap **Delete**.

## Create an event

1. Sign in, then open **Events** in the app and tap **+ Event** (bottom right).
2. Choose a cover colour with the left round button, or a photo with the right one.
3. Type the **Event name**. Then set the start and end, and tap **Choose location** to pick a site or "Other".
4. Tap **Add description** to write what it is about.
5. Under **Options**, you can set a registration link, a price, a capacity, and **Visibility**:
   - **Public** shows the event to everyone.
   - **Draft** keeps it hidden until you are ready.
6. **Pin to the top of Events** puts it in the big banner at the top (use it for one big event, such as a conference).
7. Tap **Create**.

To change an event, open it, tap **More**, then **Edit event**, and tap **Save changes**. To remove it, open **Edit event** and tap **Delete event** at the bottom.

If you leave the form before finishing, the app offers **Restore draft?** next time so you do not lose your work.

## Add a video to Watch

Only admins and leaders can add videos. Videos are YouTube links (normal videos or YouTube Shorts).

1. Upload your video to YouTube first, then copy its link.
2. Open **Watch** in the app. Tap the round **+** button at the top right.
3. Type a **Title**, paste the **YouTube link**, and tap **Publish**.
4. The new video appears first. Until a real video is added, Watch shows sample videos marked "Sample".

The name shown above the video is your **Name** on the staff list, so ask an admin to fill it in (for example "Kids Ministry").

To remove a video, open it in Watch and tap the round bin button at the top right, then confirm. Admins, staff and leaders can all remove videos.

## Book a space

Anyone can book a church space from the app, with their own email address. This is separate from the staff sign-in above.

1. Tap **+** on the Home screen, then **Book a space**. If you are not signed in yet, you are taken to **Account**. Type your email, tap **Send my code**, then type the 6-digit code from the email and tap **Sign in**. You go straight back to booking. (You can also sign in any time from the round button at the top of Home.)
2. Pick a space and a day from the pills. Swipe sideways to see more days, or tap **Other date** to jump to one.
3. Read the timeline for that day:
   - **Pink** is already booked.
   - **Grey** is requested but not yet confirmed.
   - Your own choice shows as a blue outline.
4. Tap the hours you want in the list. Tap the hour just after or just before to make it longer. Or use **Start** and **End**. If the time is already booked, the app tells you.
5. Tap **Confirm**. Fill in the **Ministry or organisation**, **What is the event?**, **Expected attendance**, and any setup or AV needs (optional).
6. Tap **Send booking request**. You will see "Request sent" (or "Booked", if it was approved straight away) with a reference number, and a confirmation goes to your email. A request is not final until the church approves it.

Your bookings are listed lower on the same page, under **Your bookings** and **Your earlier bookings**.

**Rebook:** on an earlier booking, tap **Rebook** to book the same space again. The app picks the same hours on the next same weekday and fills in your details, so you only choose a different day if you want one, then tap **Confirm** and **Send booking request**.

## Launch pop-ups

A launch pop-up is a full-screen banner that everyone sees once a day, on their first open of the day, while it is live. Tapping
it opens the linked event. Only one pop-up can be live at a time, and nothing shows to members until an admin turns the
master switch on.

**Ask for one (staff and admins):** when you create or edit a public event that has a cover photo, turn on **Request a launch
pop-up for everyone** under Options. The pop-up uses the event's photo (cut to the pop-up's tall shape, so a portrait photo works
best) and its title. It starts now and ends when the event ends; change either if you like, then save the event. Your request
goes to the people who approve pop-ups, in their Notifications. You will see the answer in your own Notifications, with a note
if it was declined. Saving the event again later does not send the request again unless you turn the switch on again.

**Approve requests (admins, and anyone with "Can approve pop-ups"):** sign in, tap **Pop-ups** on the staff page (or the request
in your Notifications). Each request shows the banner, the dates and who asked. Tap **Approve** or **Decline** (with a short note
to the requester). If another pop-up is live, approving asks you to choose: **Replace the current pop-up**, or **Keep the current
one**, which declines the new request and tells the requester why. Nobody can approve their own request.

**Run pop-ups (admins only):** on **Pop-ups**:
1. **Show to members** is the master switch. It starts off. Nothing shows to anyone until you turn it on.
2. **New pop-up**: tap **Choose banner** and pick a picture. It is cropped to the pop-up shape; slide **Position the crop** to
   choose which part shows. Give it a **Title**, link it to an event if you like, and set **Show from** and **Until**.
   **Save as draft** keeps it for later; **Make live** shows it from its start time (replacing the live one, if any).
3. **Take down** ends the live pop-up at once. A pop-up also ends by itself at its end time.

## Stories

Stories are short posts that everyone can watch from the circles at the top of Home for 24 hours, like Instagram stories.
Admins, and anyone with **Can post stories** on the staff list, can post them.

**Post one:** if you can post, your own circle is always first on Home, labelled **Your story**, with a small **+**. Tap the **+**
(or **Add story** on your profile, or **Post a story** on Account) and the camera opens straight away, full screen, like Instagram. If you have a story up, tapping
your circle plays it and the **+** still opens the camera.
- **STORY**: tap the big white button for a photo, or hold it to record a video. While holding, slide up to zoom in (down to
  zoom out), and slide left onto the lock to keep recording without holding; tap the button again to stop. Pinch the picture
  to zoom at any time, and tap it to focus there (on phones that allow it). **1x** under the picture goes back to normal, and
  **0.5x** appears on phones that offer their wide lens. What you see in the frame is exactly what is posted. The microphone
  only turns on while you record. A ring round the button fills as you record, with a mark at every 30-second part, and the
  parts build up along the top. It stops by itself after 6 parts (3 minutes). The small square at the bottom left opens your
  phone's photos and videos, the arrows at the bottom right switch between the front and back cameras, and the flash is at
  the top right.
- **VOICE**: swipe the words under the button, or tap **VOICE**. Tap the microphone to record (tap it again to stop), or hold it.
  Tap the background to change its colour, or choose a photo from your phone to go behind your voice. Up to 10 parts (5 minutes).
- **TEXT**: a colour with a cursor. Type, tap the background to change the colour, then tap the tick.
- **The tools on the left** (on the camera and on the preview): **Aa** puts words on your story, the **pen** draws, and the small
  **arrow** shows more: on the camera, **hands-free** (a tap starts and stops recording) and a **3-second timer**; on the
  preview, the **background colour**.
- **The preview**: drag words to move them, pinch (or use the mouse wheel, or the plus and minus keys) to resize them, and tap
  them to edit or recolour. The pen has colours and **undo**. The **X** discards, after asking. The **down arrow** saves the
  picture or the recording to your phone. **Your story**, with your photo on it, posts it. You can post several stories a day.
- A long video from your phone is split into 30-second parts the same way, and only the first 3 minutes are shown. Files over
  60 MB cannot be uploaded.

Good to know: on an iPhone a video picked from the phone is sent as it is (other phones make it smaller first), so keep it short.
A story's file stays available for up to two days after it is deleted or has expired, while the app itself stops showing it at once.

**Watching:** tap a circle. The bars across the top are the parts. Tap the right side for the next part, the left side for the one
before, hold to pause, swipe left or right for the next or previous person, swipe down to close. Sound is on for video and voice.

**Your stories:** your profile (the avatar on Home) shows your live stories with how many times each was watched (a number only),
and an **Add story** tile. To delete one, open Account (the gear), where each story under **Stories** has a **Delete** button,
which removes it, all parts included.

**Your name and photo:** tap **Edit profile** on your profile. If you can post stories, the display name and photo you save there
are also the ones shown on your stories. They start as your Google name and photo.

## Profiles (everyone)

Anyone with the app has a profile, signed in or not: tap the avatar at the top right of Home. It shows a banner, a photo, a name, a
bio or Bible verse, a birthday (day and month only), and counts of verses read, devotions done and the current streak.
**Edit profile** changes them; **Share my profile** sends an invitation to the app with the person's first name and the app's
address, and nothing else. Profiles stay on the phone and are never sent to the church, except that people who can post stories
also save their name and photo to the staff list for their story circle.

## Manage the staff list (admins only)

1. Sign in, then tap **Staff**.
2. To add someone, type their **Google account email**, their **Name**, and choose a **Role** (staff, leader or admin). Tap **Save**.
   The person must use that same Google account to sign in.
3. To change someone's name or role, tap them in the list, change it, and tap **Save**.
4. Tick **Can approve pop-ups** for anyone who should decide on launch pop-up requests, and **Can post stories** for anyone who
   should post stories. Admins can always do both.
5. To remove someone, tap **Delete** next to their name. You cannot delete yourself.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This account is not allowed to do that."** Your role does not include this. Check the table at the top, or ask an admin.
- **Nothing happens when you tap Save or Publish.** Check your internet connection and try again.
- **A video says it could not be loaded.** Check the link opens on YouTube and is not set to Private.
- **Anything else.** Tell the app administrator what you tapped and what you saw.
