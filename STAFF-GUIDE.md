# Life Centre app: staff guide

This guide is for church staff, leaders and admins. You do not need any technical skills.

## Who can do what

| | Admin | Staff | Leader |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Post notices | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add verses | Yes | Yes | No |
| Create and edit events | Yes | Yes | No |
| Add a video to Watch | Yes | No | Yes |
| Remove a video from Watch | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Add or remove people on the staff list | Yes | No | No |

## Sign in

1. Open the app and tap the round avatar at the top right of Home. This opens **Account**.
2. Tap **Staff sign-in with Google** and choose your Google account.
3. Account now shows your email and your role, with your tools underneath (**Admin tools** or **Staff tools**).

If it says **"Not on the staff list"**, ask an admin to add the email address you signed in with (see "Manage the staff list" below).
To leave, or to switch to another account, tap **Sign out** on Account.

To book a room you also sign in as a member (an emailed 6-digit code). Do that from **Sign in for bookings** on Account, or from Facilities booking.
The staff page is still at **https://icathia.com/lifecentre-app-prototype/app/#/staff**.

## Post a notice

Notices show in the app under the bell on the Home screen.

1. Sign in, then tap **Notices**.
2. Fill in the **Title** and the **Message**.
3. Check the **Date shown**. It is today by default.
4. If you want a button under the notice, add a **Link** (it must start with `https://`) and a **Link label** (for example "Register").
5. Tick **Published (visible to everyone)** to show it now. Leave it unticked to keep it as a draft that only staff can see.
6. Tap **Save**. You will see "Saved."

To change a notice, tap it in the list, edit it, and tap **Save**. To remove it, tap **Delete** next to it.

## Add a verse

The verse shows on the Home screen on the day you pick. If no verse is set for a day, the app shows a backup verse.

1. Sign in, then tap **Verses**.
2. Pick the **Date**, then type the **Reference** (for example "Psalm 23:1") and the **Verse**.
3. Choose **KJV** or **WEB**. These are the two translations we can show for free.
4. Tap **Save**.

To change a verse, tap it in the list, edit it, and tap **Save**. You cannot change its date; delete it and add a new one instead.

## Create an event

1. Sign in, then open **Events** in the app and tap **+ Event** (bottom right).
2. Choose a cover colour with the left round button, or a photo with the right one.
3. Type the **Event name**. Then set the start and end, and tap **Choose location** to pick a site or "Other".
4. Tap **Add description** to write what it is about.
5. Under **Options**, you can set a registration link, a price, a capacity, and **Visibility**:
   - **Public** shows the event to everyone.
   - **Draft** keeps it hidden until you are ready.
6. **Pin to the top of Events** puts it in the big banner at the top (use it for one big event, such as a conference).
7. Tap **Create**.

To change an event, open it, tap **More**, then **Edit event**, and tap **Save changes**. To remove it, open **Edit event** and tap **Delete event** at the bottom.

If you leave the form before finishing, the app offers **Restore draft?** next time so you do not lose your work.

## Add a video to Watch

Only admins and leaders can add videos. Videos are YouTube links (normal videos or YouTube Shorts).

1. Upload your video to YouTube first, then copy its link.
2. Open **Watch** in the app. Tap the round **+** button at the top right.
3. Type a **Title**, paste the **YouTube link**, and tap **Publish**.
4. The new video appears first. Until a real video is added, Watch shows sample videos marked "Sample".

The name shown above the video is your **Name** on the staff list, so ask an admin to fill it in (for example "Kids Ministry").

To remove a video, open it in Watch and tap the round bin button at the top right, then confirm. Admins, staff and leaders can all remove videos.

## Manage the staff list (admins only)

1. Sign in, then tap **Staff**.
2. To add someone, type their **Google account email**, their **Name**, and choose a **Role** (staff, leader or admin). Tap **Save**.
   The person must use that same Google account to sign in.
3. To change someone's name or role, tap them in the list, change it, and tap **Save**.
4. To remove someone, tap **Delete** next to their name. You cannot delete yourself.

## If something goes wrong

- **"This account is not allowed to do that."** Your role does not include this. Check the table at the top, or ask an admin.
- **Nothing happens when you tap Save or Publish.** Check your internet connection and try again.
- **A video says it could not be loaded.** Check the link opens on YouTube and is not set to Private.
- **Anything else.** Tell the app administrator what you tapped and what you saw.
